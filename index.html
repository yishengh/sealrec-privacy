Privacy Policy

SealRec

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Summary: SealRec is designed to work fully offline. It does not include internet permission, does not upload your recordings, and does not use advertising or analytics SDKs. Contact: sealrec@fastmail.com.

1. Who we are

SealRec (“the App”) is an Android application that records audio on your device, seals it with cryptographic hashes and a hardware-backed key, and lets you verify integrity offline.

Privacy questions: sealrec@fastmail.com.

2. Data SealRec processes

Depending on how you use the App, the following may be processed only on your device:

  • Microphone audio — captured when you start a sealed recording, stored as local sealed WAV files (and optional local verification JSON sidecars).
  • App settings — language, night mode, recording quality, notification-sound preference, stored locally.
  • Local metadata — file names, durations, key fingerprint, device-local timestamps embedded in seals, recycle-bin state.
  • Notifications — if you grant notification permission, used only to show the foreground recording service status.

The App does not create user accounts and does not collect email addresses, contacts, location history, or advertising identifiers for our use.

3. Permissions

  • Microphone — required to record audio.
  • Notifications (Android 13+) — required to show the ongoing sealed-recording foreground service.
  • Storage (legacy Android 8–9 only) — only used if you export files to shared Music/Downloads folders on older OS versions.

The App declares no network permissions (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE are not used).

4. How data is used

Local audio and seal metadata are used solely to provide recording, sealing, offline verification, rename/export, and recycle-bin features you initiate.

Device-local timestamps in seals come from the device clock and are not provided by a trusted timestamping authority (TSA).

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal data. Because SealRec has no network access in the App, recordings are not transmitted to our servers.

If you export or share a file yourself (for example via another app or email), that sharing is under your control and subject to the other app’s or recipient’s policies.

Hardware-backed cryptography uses Android platform Keystore facilities on your device; we do not operate a remote key service.

6. Retention and deletion

Recordings remain on your device until you move them to the recycle bin and permanently delete them, or uninstall the App (which removes app-private storage). Exported copies in Music/Downloads remain until you delete them from those folders.

7. Children

SealRec is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. International users

Processing occurs on your device. If you later transfer exported files across borders, you are responsible for complying with applicable laws for that transfer.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the App’s privacy practices change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised accordingly. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

For privacy requests or questions about SealRec:

sealrec@fastmail.com

隐私政策

SealRec

最近更新：2026 年 7 月 25 日

摘要：SealRec 设计为完全离线运行，不包含网络权限，不会上传你的录音，也不使用广告或分析 SDK。联系邮箱：sealrec@fastmail.com。

1. 我们是谁

SealRec（「本应用」）是一款 Android 应用：在本机录制音频，使用密码学哈希与硬件密钥密封，并可离线验真。

隐私相关问题请联系：sealrec@fastmail.com。

2. 本应用处理的数据

依你的使用方式，以下数据仅在本设备上处理：

  • 麦克风音频 — 开始密封录音时采集，保存为本地密封 WAV（以及可选的本地核验 JSON）。
  • 应用设置 — 语言、夜间模式、录音质量、录音时是否允许通知音等，均保存在本地。
  • 本地元数据 — 文件名、时长、密钥指纹、密封中的设备本地时间戳、回收站状态等。
  • 通知 — 若你授予通知权限，仅用于显示前台录音服务状态。

本应用不创建账户，也不会为运营方收集邮箱、通讯录、位置轨迹或广告标识符。

3. 权限

  • 麦克风 — 录音所必需。
  • 通知（Android 13+）— 用于显示正在进行的密封录音前台服务。
  • 存储（仅旧版 Android 8–9）— 仅在你导出到共享 Music/Downloads 目录时可能用到。

本应用不声明网络权限（不使用 INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE）。

4. 数据如何使用

本地音频与密封元数据仅用于你主动发起的录音、密封、离线鉴定、重命名/导出与回收站功能。

密封中的时间戳来自设备本地时钟，并非可信时间戳机构（TSA）提供。

5. 共享与第三方

我们不出售个人数据。因应用内无网络访问，录音不会传输到我们的服务器。

若你自行导出或通过其他应用/邮件分享文件，该行为由你控制，并受对方应用或接收方政策约束。

硬件加密使用本机 Android Keystore；我们不运营远程密钥服务。

6. 保留与删除

录音保留在本机，直到你移入回收站并彻底删除，或卸载应用（将清除应用私有存储）。导出到 Music/Downloads 的副本需你自行在对应目录删除。

7. 儿童

SealRec 不以 13 岁以下儿童（或你所在地区的同等最低年龄）为对象。我们不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。

8. 跨境

处理发生在你的设备上。若你之后将导出文件跨境传输，须自行遵守适用法律。

9. 变更

本政策可能随应用隐私实践变更而更新，上方「最近更新」日期会相应修改。更新后继续使用即表示你接受修订后的政策。

10. 联系方式
